SUBJECT: Adolescent depression
PROFILE: Child and adolescent clinical psychologist, PhD level, in outpatient practice. Treats teens with depression and comorbid anxiety. Wants to stay current on what actually helps — CBT variants, IPT-A, behavioral activation, mindfulness-based approaches, family-based treatments, digital / app-based interventions, psychopharmacology (SSRIs, safety, combination therapy). Also wants evidence on assessment tools, suicide risk screening, social-media-and-mental-health links, and school-based prevention. Cares about RCTs, meta-analyses, effect sizes, implementation science. Not interested in neuroimaging / biomarker-hunting papers, pure developmental neuroscience, or adult depression research unless it's directly applied to adolescents.
